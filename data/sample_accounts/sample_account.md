## Business & Product Summary
CloudScale Data provides real-time streaming analytics and automated observability for multi-cloud Kubernetes clusters. Their platform is deployed by mid-market and enterprise engineering teams to diagnose performance regressions and optimize cloud spending across AWS and Azure.
